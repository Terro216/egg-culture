On September 26, the pond at VDNKh’s City Farm will host Russia’s first race in giant eggshells.

About ten rowers will take part in the regatta. Each will receive a shell and a paddle, then attempt to complete the course while preserving direction, balance, and dignity.

The race will take the form of a costumed parade. Both the participants and their boats will be dressed for the occasion, leaving speed as only one of the judging criteria.

## A Boat That Already Knows Its Shape

Most boats require lengthy persuasion before they become round. The shell possesses the correct geometry from the beginning.

Before the race, each shell is lightened, its edges are finished, and its behavior on the water is tested. Only after a trial seating is it admitted to the regatta.

The largest specimens are not always selected. Excessive scale reduces maneuverability, so the chosen forms are those in which capacity has not yet defeated buoyancy.

## Inside the Dome

At the table, the [Dome](/en/guide#dome) separates the egg’s contents from the outside world. On water, its function changes direction: the environment remains outside, while the person is held within.

The shell’s cavity should not be mistaken for the [Void](/en/guide#void). The Void begins where a form can no longer hold anything. A shell boat holds a participant, a paddle, and a chosen course.

That is already a sufficiently dense content.

## The Battle of the Giants

The festival opens on September 10. From September 12 through 27, VDNKh will present an exhibition of the season’s largest specimens, open daily from 11:00 to 20:00.

The City Farm pavilion will host lectures, guided tours, and film screenings. On September 19, from 12:00 to 18:00, invited chefs will lead a culinary show alongside a demonstration of decorative shell carving.

The festival concludes with the water parade on September 26. The race will run from 15:00 to 18:00.

Until now, the shell completed the egg.

Now it must continue the journey.
